Philosophy

Built for the people who actually use it.

I don't ship portfolios. I ship products — tested, accessible, and fast. Every line of code is written for the person on the other side of the screen. No bloat. No shortcuts. No "it works on my machine."

From arcade games to grocery delivery, from e-commerce to AI automation — I build with the same obsession: clarity, craft, and care.

Process

From idea to production in 4 steps.

01

Scope & Architect

I map the real problem, not the feature list. You get a working spec, data model, and acceptance criteria — before a single pixel is built.

02

Build & Test

Production code with automated tests from day one. Every PR is reviewed, linted, and verified. No "it works on my machine" syndrome.

03

Ship & Verify

CI/CD deploys to Vercel/Firebase/Supabase. Lighthouse, accessibility, and smoke tests run automatically. You see real metrics, not promises.

04

Support & Iterate

Launch is the start, not the end. I monitor errors, gather feedback, and ship improvements. Your product stays healthy after handoff.
